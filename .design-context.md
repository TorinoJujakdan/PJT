# Design Context

## Languages
- Korean primary UI with English developer-facing naming where already present.

## Target Audience
- B2C drivers comparing fuel price, route cost, vehicle profile, and card benefits.

## Brand Personality
- Clean, trustworthy, helpful, and data-driven.

## Aesthetic Direction
- Silver & Sky Blue: Metallic/clean silver surfaces combined with refreshing sky blue accents and clear contrast backgrounds to avoid illegible gradients. We replace all mint/teal assets and variables with sky blue and silver.

## Reference Sites
- None specified.

## Accessibility Requirements
- Preserve WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states; maintain visible focus states and reduced-motion behavior.
